🎯 Problem & Solution

Problem: In college, ID Cards, Books, Notebooks and Water Bottles are lost every day. Messages in WhatsApp groups get lost and not everyone gets the information from the Security Office.

Solution: This website is a common platform where all Lost and Found items are shown in one place. Report, Search and Claim easily.

✅ Objectives

• Show Lost / Found information in one place
• Make reporting easy
• Find items quickly with Search and Filter
• Return items to the owner
• SDG 11 - Sustainable Campus Community

👥 Target Users

• Students (B.Tech All Branches)
• Teachers / Faculty
• Campus Staff / Security
• Stakeholder: NIET College Community

Claim Office: Admin Block, Ground Floor
Timing: Mon-Sat, 9 AM - 5 PM

📦 How to Claim Your Item

Step-by-Step Process

1. Find your item in Lost or Found list
2. Click on Claim This Item
3. Enter your Name and ERP ID
4. Visit Admin Block and show your College ID
5. Collect your item - Done!
